Trakt support has been removed

Native Trakt support was removed from QuickStart for compatibility with Kometa 2.4.9 and later.

QuickStart no longer stores, validates, imports, or exports Trakt credentials and native Trakt builders. Previously saved Trakt data is ignored when generating configuration files.

Supported alternatives

Use MDBList, SIMKL, FlickList, TMDb, IMDb, or another supported Kometa builder.

The mdb_trakt rating source remains available because it is provided through MDBList rather than the native Trakt integration.
